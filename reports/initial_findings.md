# Initial Findings: UK Company Risk Dashboard

## Dataset Summary

The sample dataset contains 15 UK company records.

## Company Status Breakdown

| Company Status | Count |
|---|---:|
| Active | 10 |
| Dissolved | 3 |
| Inactive | 2 |

## Risk Category Breakdown

| Risk Category | Count |
|---|---:|
| Low Risk | 7 |
| High Risk | 5 |
| Medium Risk | 3 |

## Key Observations

1. Most companies in the sample dataset are active.
2. 5 out of 15 companies are classified as high risk.
3. Dissolved and inactive companies are treated as high risk.
4. Companies with overdue accounts or overdue confirmation statements are flagged for closer review.
5. The cleaned dataset is now ready for SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard development.

## Business Interpretation

The initial analysis shows how company status and overdue filing indicators can be converted into simple risk categories.

This type of dashboard could help finance, banking, risk and operations teams quickly identify companies that may require further investigation.
