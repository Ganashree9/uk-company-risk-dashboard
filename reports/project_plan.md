# Project Plan: UK Company Risk Dashboard

## Objective

Build a data analyst portfolio project that shows how public UK company data can be cleaned, transformed and analysed to identify useful business insights for finance, banking, risk and operations teams.

## Target Audience

- Data analyst recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Finance teams
- Risk teams
- Business intelligence teams
- Operations teams

## Business Problem

Finance and operations teams often need to quickly understand company risk indicators using public company information such as company status, company age, company type and filing behaviour.

This dashboard will help users identify company groups that may require closer review.

## Key Business Questions

1. How many companies are active, dissolved or inactive?
2. What percentage of companies fall into each status category?
3. Which company types are most common?
4. How does company age relate to company status?
5. Which companies should be flagged as higher risk?
6. What KPIs should appear in an executive dashboard?

## Planned Metrics

- Total companies analysed
- Active companies
- Dissolved companies
- Inactive companies
- Company age
- Company type
- Status distribution
- Risk category
- High-risk company count
