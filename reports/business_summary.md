# Business Summary

## Project Context

This project analyses UK company data to identify company status patterns, risk indicators and business-health signals.

The final dashboard is designed for finance, banking, risk and operations stakeholders who need a quick overview of company-level risk.

## Initial Assumptions

- Dissolved or inactive companies may require closer review.
- Company age can provide useful context for business-health analysis.
- Company type and status can help segment risk.
- A dashboard should highlight both summary KPIs and detailed drill-downs.

## Expected Final Insights

The final analysis will aim to answer:

1. What proportion of companies are active, dissolved or inactive?
2. Which groups show higher-risk patterns?
3. How does company age affect risk profile?
4. What should stakeholders monitor regularly?
5. What recommendations can be made from the analysis?
